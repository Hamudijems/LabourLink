## 2025-05-14 - [Feedback and Accessibility in Multi-step Forms]
**Learning:** In flows involving async third-party verification (like Fayda ID), providing immediate, themed success feedback (using Alert components with CheckCircle icons) is crucial for orienting the user before revealing the next form section or transitioning the interface state.
**Action:** Always include a success state notification when a major async validation step completes, especially if it unlocks further parts of a form.
