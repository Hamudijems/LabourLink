## 2025-05-14 - Multi-step Verification Feedback
**Learning:** In flows involving async third-party verification (like Fayda ID), simply enabling the next section is often insufficient for clear UX. Users benefit from explicit positive reinforcement (e.g., a green success alert) to confirm that the verification succeeded and to orient them on the next required actions.
**Action:** Always pair successful async verifications with immediate, themed visual feedback (Alerts with CheckCircle icons) before moving the user to the next logical step in a form.
